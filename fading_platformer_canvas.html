Ephemeral Steps

THEME: EVERYTHING IS TEMPORARY

A World Made of Dust

A precision platformer where the environment only exists in the present. Platforms materialize as you approach and crumble into dust seconds after you touch them.

Temporary World

You can't backtrack. Hesitation leads to falling as the ground literally disappears beneath your feet.

Fading Buffs

Collect glowing embers to gain double-jump or dash abilities, but they burn out in seconds.
