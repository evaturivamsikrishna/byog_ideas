Rust & Ruin: Shifting Defense

BYOG 2026 Pitch Canvas

THEME: EVERYTHING IS TEMPORARY

Core Loop

Gather scrap from defeated enemies to constantly build and rebuild towers. Towers rust and break down after 30-60 seconds.

Temporary Mechanics

  • ⏳ Tower Decay: No defense is permanent. You must monitor tower health and replace them.
  • 🌪️ Shifting Paths: The enemy spawn points and destination randomly change every wave.
  • 🔋 Overclock: Boost a tower's power by 300%, but it destroys the tower in 5 seconds.
