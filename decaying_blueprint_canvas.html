Project: Erasure

CONFIDENTIAL BLUEPRINT PUZZLER

THEME: EVERYTHING IS TEMPORARY

Objective

Study the level architecture before the blueprint degrades. Place logic gates, traps, or character paths based entirely on your short-term memory.

Mechanics

> INK DECAY
The level map is drawn in temporary ink. You have 10 seconds to memorize the layout before it fades to a blank grid.
> BLIND EXECUTION
Once the blueprint is gone, you must place your pieces. Hitting 'Execute' runs the simulation in the dark.
