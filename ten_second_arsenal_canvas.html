TEMP-OPS: 10 SECONDS

Theme: Everything is Temporary

The Gimmick

A chaotic, top-down twin-stick shooter where your weapons are devastating but violently unstable. The moment you equip a weapon, a strict 10-second timer begins.

Mechanics

  • Use It or Lose It: Weapons disintegrate in your hands. Throw them at enemies right before they explode for bonus damage.
  • Constant Scramble: The arena drops random, temporary loot. You can never get comfortable with one playstyle.
